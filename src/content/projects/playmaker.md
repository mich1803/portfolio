**PlayMAKER** is an open-source ecosystem designed to bring practical digital tools to sports organizations of every size, competitive level, and discipline. It supports the work behind a team's public presence: publishing club information, organizing media workflows, understanding match footage, and turning sporting moments into content.

The project starts from a simple idea: useful technology should not be limited to professional organizations with large budgets or dedicated media departments. A grassroots club, a growing team, or a volunteer handling communication after practice should also be able to use adaptable tools while keeping control of their content and identity.

## One ecosystem, three tools

PlayMAKER is organized around three complementary products, each with its own development path:

- **WebMAKER** is a customizable club website and publishing workflow for stories, fixtures, results, statistics, and social content. A pilot is currently in beta testing.
- **TagMAKER** is being developed as a lightweight workflow for annotating match video with scores, timestamps, sport profiles, and reusable event data.
- **VideoMAKER** is planned as an offline-first editing workflow for creating highlights, overlays, and horizontal or vertical clips from full-game footage.

The tools are intended to remain useful on their own. Shared formats and imports are planned as the ecosystem matures, but the public website clearly distinguishes available functionality from roadmap work.

## Open and adaptable by design

The ecosystem is built around customization, modularity, and local control. Teams can begin with the tool they need, adapt presentation and workflows to their sport, and expand later without being forced into a mandatory subscription or AI service.

Explore the [official PlayMAKER website](https://playmaker-apps.github.io/) for the current product status and roadmap, or visit the [PlayMAKER organization on GitHub](https://github.com/PlayMAKER-apps) to follow the open-source work.
